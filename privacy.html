Privacy Policy

We believe privacy is a right, not a feature.

Last updated: June 2026

Short version: ChattyCHOP Inbox never stores your emails, calendar data or personal information on any server. Everything stays on your device. We only use Google's official APIs to read and manage your Gmail and Calendar — and only while you are actively using the app.

1. Who We Are

ChattyCHOP Inbox ("the app", "we", "us") is an AI-powered Gmail assistant built and maintained by G. Badrinath, a student developer based in Andhra Pradesh, India, operating under the ChattyCHOP brand.

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at:

2. What Data We Access

When you sign in with Google, ChattyCHOP Inbox requests permission to access the following:

  • Gmail (read and modify): To display your inbox, let you read emails and send replies.
  • Google Calendar (events): To show your upcoming events and let you create or delete events.
  • Basic profile (name, email, photo): To personalise your experience inside the app.

3. What We Do NOT Do

✓ No server storage ✓ No email scanning ✓ No selling data ✓ No tracking ✓ No ads
  • We never store your emails on any server. Email content is only loaded in your browser and displayed to you.
  • We never sell, share or rent your personal data to any third party.
  • We never use your emails for advertising or to train any AI model.
  • We never read your emails outside of what you actively open in the app.
  • We do not use any analytics trackers like Google Analytics or Meta Pixel.
  • We do not send you marketing emails of any kind.

4. How Your Data Is Stored

ChattyCHOP Inbox is a 100% client-side application. This means:

  • Your chats, notes, to-dos and memory are stored in your browser's localStorage on your own device only.
  • Your Google access token is stored temporarily in your browser and is never sent to our servers.
  • App configuration (message limits, pro codes, announcements) is stored in a JSONBin database. This contains no personal user data — only app settings.
  • If you clear your browser data, all locally stored information is permanently deleted.

We do not operate any backend server that receives or processes your personal information. There is no database of users, no account system and no password.

5. AI and Groq API

ChattyCHOP Inbox uses Groq's API to power the AI chat and email drafting features. When you send a message to the AI:

  • Your message and recent inbox context is sent to Groq's servers to generate a response.
  • Groq may process this data according to their own Privacy Policy.
  • We recommend avoiding sharing sensitive personal information (passwords, financial details) in AI chat.
  • Email content sent to the AI is only what you explicitly ask about — we do not automatically feed your entire inbox to the AI.

6. Google API Compliance

ChattyCHOP Inbox's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • We only use Google data to provide and improve the features you requested within the app.
  • We do not use Google data to serve you ads.
  • We do not allow humans (including us) to read your Gmail data unless required by law or you explicitly request support.
  • Google data is never used to train AI or machine learning models.

7. Pro and Premium Subscriptions

If you purchase a Pro or Premium plan:

  • Payments are made directly via UPI to a personal account and are processed entirely outside the app.
  • We do not collect or store any payment card information.
  • Your Gmail address is used to generate and validate your access code. This is stored in our app configuration only.
  • Payment screenshots you share for verification are only used to confirm your payment and are not stored in any public database.

8. Children's Privacy

ChattyCHOP Inbox is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

9. Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Delete your data: Clear your browser's localStorage and all app data is gone instantly.
  • Revoke access: Go to myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove ChattyCHOP Inbox at any time.
  • Sign out: Click Sign Out in the app sidebar — your Google token is immediately cleared.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

For significant changes, we will notify users through the in-app announcement system.
