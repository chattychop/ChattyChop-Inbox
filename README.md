# ChattyChop-Inbox
An AI-powered email assistant built by G. Badrinath from Hyderabad, India. Connect your Gmail, manage your inbox smarter, get AI-drafted replies, and stay on top of your calendar — all in one place.
Features
Sign in with Google to instantly connect your Gmail inbox. The AI reads your emails, suggests replies, detects urgent messages, and helps you clean up junk. You also get a built-in calendar view, to-do list, notes, smart labels, and follow-up reminders.
The AI assistant works like a real chat — ask it anything about your emails, your schedule, or your work. It remembers your name, the people you mention, and any instructions you give it across sessions.
Every tier gets full access to AI replies, humanize, AI checker, smart inbox, calendar, to-dos, notes, and smart clean.

Tech
Built with React, Gmail API, Google Calendar API, and Google OAuth. No backend, no server — runs entirely in your browser. AI powered by Groq with Llama 3.3 70b.
